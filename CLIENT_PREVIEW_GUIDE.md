# 🎨 Client Preview Guide - brainboxX Theme System

## Quick Start

### View Your Website
1. Open **marketplace.html** in your web browser
2. Or open **brainboxtools.html** to see the main homepage

### Try the Theme Toggle
1. **Click the button** in the top-right corner of the header (sun icon = light mode, moon icon = dark mode)
2. **Or press Shift+D** on your keyboard to instantly switch themes
3. **Refresh the page** - your preference is saved!
